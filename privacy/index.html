Lucia

Privacy.

Lucia has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of ours. Your photographs stay on your devices. This page lists the few places where data leaves them, and why.

Who is responsible

Lucia is made by Mario Hainzl, Lendkai 31, 8020 Graz, Austria. Questions about anything on this page: hello@luciacam.app.

Your photographs

Negatives, prints, clips and their recipes are stored on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and on drives you connect. Lucia never uploads them anywhere. We cannot see them.

Lucia Link

Your devices talk to each other directly over your local network, encrypted, after you pair them with a code. Nothing in that exchange passes through a server.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Lucia keeps ratings, edits, crops, place names and your list of presets and looks in your own private iCloud account, so they match on all your devices. This is on by default. Photographs are never part of it. The data sits with Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

You can turn it off for Lucia in your device’s iCloud settings, under the apps that use iCloud. Lucia can also delete everything it stored there: Remove Lucia Data from iCloud.

Location

With your permission, Lucia saves where a photograph was taken, inside the photograph’s own file, and centers the map on where you are. The location stays on your devices and in your private iCloud, as described above.

The map

The map tiles come from MapTiler. When you open the map, your device asks MapTiler for the area you are looking at. MapTiler therefore sees your IP address and which part of the map was requested. It does not get your photographs or their exact positions.

Camera, microphone, photo library

Lucia uses the camera to take pictures and the microphone when you record video. It reads RAW files from your photo library when you import them, and saves prints there when you export. None of this leaves your device.

TestFlight

While Lucia is in beta, it is distributed through Apple’s TestFlight. If you agree in TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you send with us. Apple’s own privacy terms apply to TestFlight.

This website

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the page.
  • Beta list. If you leave your email address, it is sent through FormSubmit to our inbox, together with whether you clicked iPhone or Mac. We use it for one thing: to tell you when the beta is online. Write to us and we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use. In practice that is your email address, if you gave it to us. Write to hello@luciacam.app. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Austria that is the Datenschutzbehörde.
