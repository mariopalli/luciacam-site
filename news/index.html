Lucia has a second home.

The biggest update so far. Lucia is no longer just an iPhone app: it now has a home on your Mac and iPad, a new video engine, a lot more camera, and a library that takes care of itself. Thank you to everyone who tested and sent feedback and pictures. Please keep sending images!

Lucia RAW Studio for Mac and iPad

Lucia RAW Studio shares everything with your iPhone: the same library, the same looks, the same edits, the same presets.

  • View, rate and edit every shot on a big screen, full screen if you like.
  • Library in rows that keep each photo’s real shape, or as classic tiles.
  • Sidebar by year, month and day, plus your devices and drives.
  • A map of all your photos. Tap a cluster and swipe through just those.
  • Custom albums. Drag photos straight into them, from the library, the map or the editor, and put them in any order you like.
  • Import from your iPhone or from a drive. Lucia knows what is new and never imports a photo twice.
  • Export with passepartout, Instagram canvas and the format of your choice. Or copy an image to the clipboard and paste it anywhere.
  • Keyboard first. Hold E, S, H, C, K or W and use the arrows for exposure, tone curve, color, Kelvin and WB shift. P and the arrows run through your presets. Tab shows the keys right next to the menu rows, and every key can be changed.
  • A proper menu bar on Mac and iPad: File, Edit, Photo, View.
  • Library Overview: one number for your whole library, and exactly where every photo lives.
  • Tethering: shoot on your iPhone and the photo lands on your Mac.

Lucia Link

Pair once with a code. After that iPhone, Mac and iPad find each other on your Wi-Fi, or directly when there is no network around.

  • New photos, ratings and edits go back and forth by themselves.
  • Edits on the Mac show up on the iPhone, even in an open editor.
  • One pop-up tells you what will happen. No chain of questions.
  • While syncing, your iPhone shows a calm Link screen with a progress bar and STOP TRANSFER. Your Mac shows it in the menu bar.
  • Several Lucia devices in the same network work together.

Your negatives have a home

Negatives are big. Your iPhone shouldn’t have to carry all of them.

  • Your Mac or iPad is the home of your negatives. It keeps all of them.
  • Your iPhone keeps a storage budget: Small 15 GB, Medium 30 GB, Large 90 GB or No limit. Lucia picks a sensible one and tells you.
  • Recent and highly rated photos stay on the iPhone the longest.
  • A negative only leaves your iPhone once a safe copy exists. Prints, stars and edits always stay.
  • Open a photo whose negative is elsewhere and Lucia fetches it from your Mac or iPad in a few seconds.
  • Small marks tell you where a negative lives: ON MAC, ON IPAD, ON DRIVE.

Backup on the go

On the road without a Mac? Connect a compatible SSD to your iPhone or iPad.

  • BACKUP NOW copies your negatives to the drive and frees up space.
  • Folders by year, month and day, named after the place you shot: 2026-10-07 Zadar.
  • Every photo travels as negative, print and recipe, so the drive is a complete library on its own.
  • Ratings and recipes are matched both ways when you plug in.
  • The drive icon pulses red as long as something isn’t backed up yet.

Video, rebuilt

Video got its own engine, its own library and its own editor.

  • Lucia records a 4K Apple Log master and grades it afterwards. Your look is never burned in.
  • 24, 25, 30, 50, 60 and 120 fps with 180 degree shutter. Open Gate 4:3 on supported lenses.
  • Record straight to an external drive. The header shows the minutes left.
  • Stereo sound at 48 kHz.
  • Clip editor with the same menu as photos: presets, films, white balance, black point, lens correction, grain.
  • Slow motion playback and export, with sound.
  • Export graded as HEVC, or as Log: Apple Log, F-Log, F-Log2 or S-Log3, ready for your grading software.
  • Live histogram in the viewfinder. Swipe up or down.

Camera

  • 14 mm and 50 mm. Tap the selected lens a second time: 18 becomes 14, 35 becomes 50. Lucia remembers your choice.
  • The 50 mm is a real sensor crop, so video at 50 mm is true 4K.
  • Grid drawer with thirds, center cross and LEVEL, a horizon line that clicks into place when you’re straight.
  • STRAIGHTEN AUTO uses the angle at the exact moment you pressed the shutter. Up to 15 degrees.
  • Faces only take over the focus when they are big enough in the frame.
  • Lucia has its own shutter click.
  • C0 RAW: your photo without any look, as a clean starting point.
  • INFO shows the real megapixels of every photo.

Editing and library

  • Paste Settings now carries exposure along, for one photo or many at once.
  • The PRESET row turns red when your settings match a preset exactly.
  • FILMS › Get Fujifilm Film Simulations opens Fujifilm’s free download.
  • Instagram export as a full 9:16 canvas: the story is filled, the feed crop stays the same.
  • Export failed now tells you why and how to fix it.
  • Lucia pins Apple’s RAW decoder, so your photos look the same after an iOS update.
  • The library opens faster, and filters keep your place instead of jumping to the top.
  • Trash keeps deleted photos for 30 days. Delete here, or everywhere.

Fixed

  • A crash with strong grain.
  • Black or partly black photos after pasting settings.
  • Photos that had disappeared from the iPhone library.
  • A banding edge in skies with RAW BOOST.
  • A white flash on launch. Lucia starts black now.
  • Clips that went black after a few seconds with a look.
  • Many smaller things on the way.

Good to know

  • Lucia needs iOS 18 now.
  • Negatives no longer show up in the Files app. Mac, iPad and SSD are the way now.
  • Clips stay in Lucia. Export them to get them into Photos.
  • iOS asks for access to your local network. Please allow it, otherwise pairing and sync can’t work.
  • iCloud syncs ratings, edits, presets and albums. Never your photos.
  • After the update your library is prepared once. All photos and presets are safe, it just takes a moment.
